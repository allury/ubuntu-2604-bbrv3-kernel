Method
------
Google's change is the diff from v6.13.7 to bbrv3-2025-03-18. The port's change
is the diff from Ubuntu-7.0.0-30.30 to that tag with the patch applied. Both are
taken without context lines and compared line by line after collapsing
whitespace; blank lines are ignored. A line counts as matched when the
other change has the same line with the same sign in the same file, or
failing that, for lines of at least 10 characters, in another file. Files
one change creates and the other does not touch are listed, not compared.
Whole files are compared as final content, together with the Linux
changes to them between the two bases that the port had to carry over.
